Frequently asked questions

How do I add a recipe?

Tap Add recipe. Choose Paste recipe to import a link or copied text, or Write recipe to type one yourself. Imported recipes open for review before you save them.

How many recipes can I import?

You can import 3 recipes a month from links or pasted text; the allowance renews at the start of each month. Writing recipes yourself is always free and unlimited.

Why did an import fail?

Some pages block apps from reading them or don't contain a full recipe. Copy the ingredients and preparation from the page and use Paste recipe with the text instead.

How do I change the language or the units?

Open Settings. Under Language choose English, Deutsch or עברית — your saved recipes are translated too (this needs an internet connection the first time). Under Measurements choose metric or US units; the original amount is always shown next to the converted one.

Where are my recipes stored? Are they backed up?

Your recipes, photos and shopping list are stored only on your iPhone. Mana does not back them up or sync them between devices yet, so deleting the app deletes your cookbook.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a recipe from its page, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for the little data our server keeps and how to have it removed.
