MANA - YOUR RECIPES, BEAUTIFULLY ORGANIZED
==========================================

What it is
----------
Mana is a personal cookbook app for iPhone and Android. It collects the recipes
you find anywhere - on websites, in messages, in notes, or in your head - and
turns them into one clean, consistent cookbook in your own language.

The app is available in English, German and Hebrew (with full right-to-left
layout for Hebrew).


Main features
-------------
1. Import a recipe from a web link
   Paste the link to a recipe page. Mana reads only the recipe from the page -
   title, servings, times, ingredients and preparation steps - and leaves out
   ads, stories and comments. If the recipe is in another language, it is
   translated into yours; if it is already in your language, it is imported
   instantly without any AI processing.

2. Import pasted recipe text
   Copy recipe text from anywhere and paste it. Mana recognises the
   ingredients and steps, cleans them up and translates them if needed.

3. Faithful translation
   Quantities, units, temperatures, times and the source are kept exactly as
   written. Nothing is invented: if information is missing or unclear, Mana
   shows a "Please check these details" note instead of guessing.

4. Review before saving
   Every imported recipe opens in an editable review screen. You can fix the
   title, servings, times, ingredients and steps, reorder them, and choose a
   category and tags before saving.

5. Write your own recipes
   Add family recipes or favourites from memory with the built-in editor.

6. Organised cookbook
   Thirteen categories (starters, soups, salads, main courses, side dishes,
   pasta & rice, breakfast, baking, desserts, snacks, sauces & dips, drinks,
   other), tags, a "recently added" shelf and fast search.

7. Favourites
   Mark the recipes you love and find them in their own tab.

8. Share
   Send any recipe as nicely formatted text through the phone's share menu.

9. Private by design
   Your cookbook is stored on your phone and works offline. Only the link or
   text you choose to import is sent to the recipe service; your cookbook is
   never uploaded. No registration is required.

10. Recipe photos
    Link imports keep the photo from the recipe website, and you can take a
    photo of the dish you made (or choose one from your library) for any recipe.

11. Light and dark mode.


How it works (in short)
-----------------------
- The app stores recipes locally on the device.
- Imports go to a secure Mana server function, which reads the recipe page and,
  when translation is needed, uses Claude (by Anthropic) to structure and
  translate only the recipe content.
- Each person can make up to 30 imports per day.
